Fractional CIO

Fractional CIO leadership for organizations that need senior IT judgment now

A fractional CIO from Condor brings Fortune 500 caliber information technology leadership to your organization on a part-time basis: strategy, governance, vendors, security and the modernization work that keeps the business running well.

What a fractional CIO does

Where a CTO usually leads the technology a company sells, a CIO leads the technology a company runs on: core systems, data, infrastructure, security, vendors and the IT organization. A fractional CIO provides that leadership part-time, with full accountability for the outcomes.

Condor has led information technology at Fortune 500 scale and inside PE and VC-backed growth companies. That range matters, because the right answer for a growing company is rarely the enterprise playbook copied down, and rarely an improvised one either.

What is included

  • IT strategy and a multi-year roadmap aligned to business goals and budget
  • Governance, IT operating model and the right structure for in-house and outsourced teams
  • Vendor and contract strategy, including consolidation and renegotiation
  • Security, compliance and risk management appropriate to your industry
  • Modernization of legacy systems and cloud migration planning
  • Integration planning for acquisitions and carve-outs
  • Practical AI adoption across operations, with guardrails and measurement

When a fractional CIO fits

Organizations bring in a fractional CIO when IT has grown up around the business without a plan, when an acquisition or carve-out needs systems leadership, when costs and vendors have sprawled, or when the board wants a clear view of technology risk. A fractional CIO is also a strong bridge while a permanent hire is found.

Common questions

What is a fractional CIO?

A fractional CIO is an experienced chief information officer who leads an organization's IT strategy, systems, security and vendors part-time on an ongoing basis, with accountability for outcomes.

What is the difference between a fractional CIO and a fractional CTO?

A CIO focuses on the technology the business runs on, such as core systems, data, infrastructure and vendors. A CTO focuses on the technology the business builds and sells. Condor provides both, and many growing companies need a blend.

Can a fractional CIO help with an acquisition?

Yes. Condor leads technology through acquisitions, integrations and carve-outs, including systems consolidation and the plan that holds after close.
