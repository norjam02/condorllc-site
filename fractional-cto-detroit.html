Technology leadership for Southeast Michigan companies

Southeast Michigan's economy runs on manufacturing, mobility, healthcare, financial services and a growing base of software and PE-backed companies. Many of those businesses are modernizing systems built for a different era while adopting AI and digital products at the same time. That combination calls for senior technology leadership, and Condor provides it as a fractional CTO, fractional CIO or head of product, or advises on a defined problem.

We work with companies across the region, including Detroit, Ann Arbor, Novi, Northville, Livonia, Troy, Southfield and Farmington Hills, and meet in person for leadership sessions, board meetings and team planning.

Services for Detroit area companies

  • Fractional CTO: technology strategy, engineering leadership and delivery ownership
  • Fractional CIO: IT strategy, governance, vendors, security and modernization of legacy systems
  • Fractional product leadership: product market position and focus on the highest value work
  • Technology consulting: technology strategy, AI strategy and transformation
  • Healthcare technology: a deep specialty for the region's health systems and health IT companies

Common questions

Is there a fractional CTO in the Detroit area who can work on-site?

Yes. Condor works on-site with companies across metro Detroit and Southeast Michigan when the work calls for it, and remotely in between.

Do you work with manufacturing and automotive companies?

Condor's experience centers on healthcare, SaaS and enterprise platforms. The same leadership discipline applies to modernization and AI adoption in manufacturing and mobility businesses, and we will tell you plainly in a first conversation whether we are the right fit.

Do you only work with Michigan companies?

No. Condor works with companies nationally and globally. Southeast Michigan clients simply get easy in-person access.
