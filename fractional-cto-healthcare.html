Healthcare Technology

Fractional CTO leadership for healthcare technology companies

Healthcare technology is one of Condor's deepest specialties. We bring fractional CTO, CIO and product leadership to digital health, health IT and healthcare services companies, where compliance, interoperability and trust are part of every decision.

Home / Services / Healthcare Technology

Why healthcare needs a different kind of technology leader

Healthcare technology carries constraints most industries do not: protected health information, HIPAA and security obligations, payer and provider integrations, interoperability standards, and buyers who move carefully. A technology leader who has not worked inside those constraints learns them on your time. Condor has led technology and product in healthcare at Fortune 500 scale and inside PE and VC-backed healthcare technology companies.

Where we help

  • Platform strategy and modernization for health IT and digital health products
  • Interoperability and data exchange, including standards-based integrations
  • HIPAA, security and compliance posture that supports growth and enterprise sales
  • Responsible AI adoption with healthcare data, guardrails and measurement
  • Engineering and product leadership through growth, acquisitions and new sponsors

Beyond healthcare

Healthcare is a deep specialty, not a limit. The same leadership discipline applies across SaaS and enterprise platforms, and Condor works across both.

Common questions

Do you only work with healthcare companies?

No. Healthcare technology is a deep specialty, and Condor also works with SaaS, enterprise platform and other technology-enabled companies.

Can a fractional CTO help with HIPAA compliance?

Yes. Condor sets the security and compliance posture appropriate to your stage, working alongside your legal and compliance advisors, so compliance supports growth rather than slowing it.

Do you work with PE-backed healthcare companies?

Yes. Condor has led technology inside PE and VC-backed healthcare technology companies and understands sponsor expectations around growth and value creation.
