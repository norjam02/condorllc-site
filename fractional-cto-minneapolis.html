Technology leadership for Twin Cities companies

The Twin Cities have a deep base of healthcare, medical technology, financial services, retail and enterprise companies, plus a growing community of PE and VC-backed businesses. Many of them reach a point where technology needs senior leadership before a full-time executive makes sense. Condor fills that seat as a fractional CTO, fractional CIO or head of product, or advises on a defined problem.

We work with companies across the metro, including Minneapolis, St. Paul, Edina, Minnetonka, Bloomington, Eden Prairie and Plymouth, and meet in person for the moments that need it: leadership offsites, board meetings, team planning and hard conversations.

Services for Minneapolis companies

  • Fractional CTO: technology strategy, engineering leadership and delivery ownership
  • Fractional CIO: IT strategy, governance, vendors, security and modernization
  • Fractional product leadership: product market position and focus on the highest value work
  • Technology consulting: technology strategy, AI strategy and transformation
  • Healthcare technology: a deep specialty that fits the region's health and medtech base

Common questions

Is there a fractional CTO in Minneapolis who can work on-site?

Yes. Condor's founder is based in the Twin Cities and works on-site with Minneapolis and St. Paul companies when the work calls for it, and remotely in between.

Do you only work with Minnesota companies?

No. Condor works with companies nationally and globally. Twin Cities clients simply get easy in-person access.

What does a fractional CTO cost in Minneapolis?

Condor engagements are typically a monthly retainer sized to the level of involvement you need. We scope it together in a first conversation.
