Fractional CTO

Fractional CTO services that own the outcome

A fractional CTO from Condor steps into the technology leadership seat, sets direction, and is accountable for delivery, for a fraction of the cost and commitment of a full-time executive hire.

What a fractional CTO does

A fractional CTO is an experienced chief technology officer who leads your technology function on a part-time, ongoing basis. The role is not advisory only. Condor takes ownership of the technology strategy, the engineering organization and the delivery commitments that come with them, and reports on them plainly to the CEO, the board or the sponsor.

Most companies bring in a fractional CTO when the technology needs senior judgment now, but a full-time executive is not yet the right answer, or the search for one is taking longer than the business can wait.

What is included

  • Technology strategy and a roadmap tied to where the business is going, not just where it is today
  • Engineering leadership: team structure, hiring, delivery cadence and the operating model that keeps commitments
  • Architecture and platform decisions, including build versus buy and vendor selection
  • AI maturity and adoption built into how engineering and product teams actually work
  • Security, compliance and risk posture appropriate to your stage and industry
  • Clear reporting to leadership, boards and investors on technology outcomes

When a fractional CTO fits

A fractional CTO tends to pay for itself at inflection points: growth has outpaced the platform and the team, a CTO has left and the search is underway, a sponsor needs a credible operator at the technology layer after an investment, or a capable team needs direction and a working operating model. If none of these sound familiar, we will tell you so.

Fractional CTO, interim CTO or consultant

A consultant recommends. A fractional CTO decides and delivers. An interim CTO is a full-time placement for a defined period, usually while a permanent hire is found. Condor works in both fractional and interim models, and many engagements start fractional and adjust as needs change.

Common questions

What is a fractional CTO?

A fractional CTO is an experienced chief technology officer who leads a company's technology function part-time on an ongoing basis. Unlike an advisor, a fractional CTO owns technology strategy, engineering leadership and delivery outcomes.

How are fractional CTO engagements structured?

Condor engagements are typically a monthly retainer for a set level of involvement, sized to the work in front of you and adjusted as needs change. There are no long lock-in terms.

Do you work with companies outside your home region?

Yes. Condor works with companies nationally and globally, remotely and on-site as the work requires.

What industries do you work in?

Condor works across SaaS, enterprise platforms and technology-enabled businesses, with deep experience in healthcare technology.
