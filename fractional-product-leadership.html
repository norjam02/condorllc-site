Fractional Product Leadership

Fractional product leadership that puts the highest value work first

Condor provides fractional chief product officer and head of product leadership: sharpening your product market position, aligning product and engineering, and making sure teams are working on what matters most.

Home / Services / Fractional Product Leadership

What fractional product leadership does

A fractional CPO or head of product leads the product function part-time: deciding what to build and why, connecting the roadmap to revenue and customer outcomes, and building the operating model that turns plans into shipped software. Because Condor leads both product and technology, the two functions stop pulling in different directions.

What is included

  • Product market position and a clear point of view on where to win
  • Prioritization that puts the highest value work first, and the discipline to leave the rest alone
  • Roadmaps tied to measurable outcomes, using objectives and key results where they help
  • Product and engineering alignment, delivery cadence and release practices
  • Building or coaching the product team, including hiring product managers
  • AI-enabled product opportunities assessed on real customer value, not hype

When it fits

Fractional product leadership fits when the roadmap keeps shifting, when engineering is busy but outcomes are not moving, when a founder needs to step back from day-to-day product decisions, or when a new sponsor wants a sharper product story and the plan to deliver it.

Common questions

What is a fractional CPO?

A fractional chief product officer leads a company's product strategy, prioritization and product team part-time on an ongoing basis, accountable for product outcomes rather than only advice.

Can Condor lead product and technology together?

Yes. Condor leads both functions, which keeps product decisions and engineering delivery aligned under one accountable leader.

How do you decide what to build first?

We start with an honest assessment of the market position, customers and economics, then rank work by value and effort so the team ships the highest value items first.
