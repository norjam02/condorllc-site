When Companies Call Us

The moments where experienced leadership pays for itself

Condor tends to be most valuable at specific inflection points, when the cost of getting technology leadership wrong is high and the time to get it right is short.

Growth has outpaced the technology organization

The business is scaling faster than the platform and the team can keep up with, and delivery is starting to slow at exactly the wrong moment.

A leadership gap is creating drag

A CTO departure, a search that is taking longer than planned, or an engineering and product function that has never had senior ownership.

A sponsor needs a credible operator

Private equity sponsors and boards who need someone accountable at the technology layer, before or after an investment, who can report clearly and act on the plan.

A capable team needs direction

The people are good, but priorities are unclear, the roadmap keeps shifting, and nobody has set the operating model that lets the team execute.
