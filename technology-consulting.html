How our technology consulting is different

Condor is not a strategy firm that hands you a slide deck and leaves. Our advice comes from having led technology and product organizations through growth, acquisitions and modernization, and we stay close enough to the work to see it through. Every engagement starts by understanding what is actually broken before proposing anything.

Technology strategy

Platform architecture, build versus buy decisions, vendor evaluation and a roadmap grounded in where the business is going. The result is a plan your team can execute and your board can follow.

AI strategy and adoption

AI is an accelerator, not a replacement. We help organizations assess AI maturity, identify where AI creates real operational leverage, put guardrails and measurement in place, and build adoption into how engineering, product and operations teams work every week, without the hype traps that waste time and budget.

Engineering modernization

Structured programs that reduce technical debt, migrate to cloud-native architecture and restore engineering velocity without halting product delivery.

Transformation and integration

Acquisitions, re-platforming, market pivots and post-investment buildouts, led with the operating discipline that makes change hold.

Common questions

What does a technology consultant do?

A technology consultant helps an organization make and execute technology decisions: strategy, architecture, vendors, AI adoption and modernization. Condor's advice comes from having run technology and product organizations, and we stay accountable for results.

Do you offer AI strategy consulting?

Yes. Condor assesses AI maturity, prioritizes use cases by value, and builds AI adoption into how engineering and product teams operate, with guardrails and measurement.

Can a consulting engagement become fractional leadership?

Yes. Many clients start with a focused advisory engagement and move to fractional CTO or CIO leadership when ongoing ownership is needed.
